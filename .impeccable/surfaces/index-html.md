## Direction contract

THESIS: Trust here is earned by an editorial hand, not a wellness template. Quiet Press refuses the soft-gradient, rounded-icon-card, pastel-cream "calm app" default and instead reads like a small, deliberate printed feature — real photography, confident type, and quiet space doing the reassuring, not decoration.

OWN-WORLD: Warm ink-on-paper ground (deep cream, not pastel), near-black ink text, one carried accent — a muted clay/terracotta used sparingly as a persistent "spine" (underline rules, active nav state, form focus, the crisis banner's edge) across every page. A serif display voice carries editorial weight; a plain warm sans carries body and UI. Photography is cropped and placed like printed feature spreads, full-bleed plates, never a hero banner with a gradient scrim. No icon-plus-heading card grid as page structure; content reads as passages and full-bleed images.

STORY: An anxious first-time visitor meets one honest photograph and a plainly stated first line, hears Jennifer's own voice rather than marketing copy, reads "how I work" as short editorial passages (not a 4-up icon grid), reads real testimonials as quoted excerpts in pull-quote typography (not a carousel of identical cards), and books through a form staged as a visible, legible step sequence.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Type-led, no stock room photo (hero-room.jpeg dropped as generic AI-stock interior imagery, by user decision). Large serif headline is the dominant compositional mass directly on the warm paper ground, generous measure, one-line lede beneath. Primary action is an understated inline text-link button carrying the clay spine color, not a pill or gradient CTA; secondary action is a plain underlined text link. The clay spine rule runs under the nav as the first instance of the persistent identity system. A modest real-photo detail (cropped from therapy.jpg) may appear as a small supporting plate, never dominant or full-bleed.

FORM: Assigned direction, candidate 5 of 7 on the resonance-ordered list (small-press/indie-zine editorial calm), seed key d98e063b. Raised past four declined challengers: a persistent color spine as cross-page identity (from an endurance-livery stripe system), one singular bold focal composition in the first viewport rather than scattered motifs (from a synthwave grid-horizon world), the booking flow rendered as a visible, legible step sequence (from a jacquard-weave traceability system), and every repeated element (testimonial quotes, service passages) sharing one exact measured rhythm (from a VU-meter row system). Absorbed one competitive challenger: hierarchy carried through type scale and weight contrast alone, confident display letterforms doing real trust-building work (from a variable-font specimen world).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
